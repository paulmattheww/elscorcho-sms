El Scorcho Household Alerts — last updated July 24, 2026

  1. Program description. El Scorcho Household Alerts, sent from (970) 423-2661, delivers weekly meal plan summaries, grocery order status updates, and shopping substitution questions by SMS/MMS to the two members of the program owner's household.
  2. Opt-in. Subscribers opt in by texting START to (970) 423-2661, as described on the program page. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.
  3. Message frequency. Message frequency varies; typically 2–10 messages per week, concentrated on weekends.
  4. Fees. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.
  5. Opt-out. You can cancel at any time by texting STOP. After you send STOP, you will receive one final message confirming your unsubscription. No further messages will be sent unless you opt back in.
  6. Help. Text HELP at any time, or contact paulmattheww@gmail.com.
  7. Carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

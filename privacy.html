Privacy Policy

El Scorcho Household Alerts — last updated July 24, 2026

El Scorcho Household Alerts is a private, personal SMS program operated by its owner for their own household. It sends meal planning and grocery shopping messages to exactly two recipients: the program owner and their spouse.

Information we collect

The program stores only the names and mobile phone numbers of its two household members, and the content of messages exchanged with them. No information is collected from anyone else, and no cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies are used.

How information is used

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver meal plan summaries, grocery order updates, and shopping substitution questions to the household, and to read the household's replies.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Message delivery is processed by our SMS provider (Twilio Inc.) acting solely as a service provider.

Opt-out

Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help.
